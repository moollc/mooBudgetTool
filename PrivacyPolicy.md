# mBT Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 21, 2026

The Moo Budget Tool (mBT) is built to be private by default. 

### 1. Your Data Stays with You
Normally, everything you do in mBT stays right on your device (in your browser's memory). 
* **We cannot see your work.** 
* If you clear your browser's "site data" or history for mBT, your projects will be deleted (unless you saved them as a file or used Cloud Sync).

### 2. Optional Cloud Sync
If you turn on "Cloud Sync" in Settings:
* Your data is sent securely to our cloud server so you can access it from other devices.
* We use high-security settings to make sure only you can see your data.
* You can turn this off and delete your cloud data whenever you want.

### 3. AI Features
mBT has AI tools to help you analyze your budget or chat with a bot.
* **Local Analytics**: The basic analysis tool runs entirely on your own computer. No data is sent over the internet.
* **External AI (OpenAI, Claude, etc.)**: If you use your own API key to chat with a bot:
    * The tool sends parts of your project (like budget totals or names) to that AI company so they can answer you.
    * We don't control how those companies use your data. 
    * **LM Studio**: If you use a local AI server (LM Studio), no data leaves your machine.

### 4. No Spying or Tracking
We don't use cookies to track you, and we don't use "analytics" scripts to watch what you do. We have no idea who you are or what you're working on unless you email us for help.

### 5. No Hidden Connections
The app is self-contained. All the code needed to run it is included inside the folder. It doesn't need to "call home" or download extra pieces from the internet to work.
