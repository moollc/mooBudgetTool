1. **Check Your Numbers**: This tool is for estimating film costs. You are 100% responsible for the accuracy of your final budget. We are not responsible for any money lost or mistakes in the math.

2. **AI Can Be Wrong**: The AI features (Assistant, Analytics, Auto-Fill) are just helpers. AI can sometimes "make stuff up" or give wrong advice. Never trust an AI suggestion (like a rate or a calculation) without checking it yourself.

3. **Your Data & Privacy**: 
   * By default, your work stays on your own computer or phone.

4. **Not a Professional Service**: mBT is a tool, not a professional accountant or lawyer. Use it to help you work, but talk to a real pro before you sign off on a final production budget.
